# Tempo Tab (`/tempo`)

> **For Claude:** Read this file before making any changes to the Tempo Trainer. Every feature listed here must still work after your edits.

Audio + visual tempo cues for putting, chipping, and full swing. Client-only — no Google Sheets reads or writes.

---

## Tempos

Presets are frame counts of 30fps video (Tour Tempo convention): `back/down` = frames from takeaway to top / top to impact. 1 frame = 33.3 ms.

| Mode | Ratio | Presets | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| Putting | 2:1 | 14/7, 16/8, 18/9, 20/10, 22/11, 24/12 | 18/9 |
| Chipping | 2:1 | 14/7, 16/8, 18/9, 20/10, 22/11, 24/12 | 18/9 |
| Full swing | 3:1 | 18/6, 21/7, 24/8, 27/9, 30/10 | 24/8 |

Putting also has a **Metronome** cue: 60–90 BPM (default 76). Takeaway on the low tick, impact on the high tick, one beat of rest, repeat.

Defined in `lib/tempo.ts` (pure, unit-tested in `lib/__tests__/tempo.test.ts`).

## Layout

- **Shot type** segmented control: Putting / Chipping / Full swing
- **Putting cue** segmented control (putting only): 3-tone / Metronome
- **Visual card**
  - Current preset (or BPM) in large mono type, with ms back / ms down and ratio
  - **Swing dial** (`components/tempo/swing-dial.tsx`): a ring with a face-on golfer silhouette in the middle
    - Ring is laid out like the swing — Start · Impact at 6 o'clock, Top at 12, with a tick at each
    - Backswing fills the left half (6 → 12, arriving on the top tone); downswing fills the right half (12 → 6, closing the circle on the impact tone). The right half fills 2× / 3× faster, showing the ratio
    - Impact: full ring glows briefly
    - Rest: the full ring turns muted and unwinds to empty as the next rep approaches; same during the lead-in after Start. 3-tone shows a "Next rep in N.Ns" countdown underneath; metronome unwinds over its one-beat rest with no countdown
    - Empty when stopped and after the rep when Repeat is off
    - Golfer: video frames from a sprite sheet per shot type, drawn on a `<canvas>` over the ring and tinted with the theme's foreground colour. The dial loads only the selected shot type's sheet. `spriteFrame()` in `lib/tempo.ts` picks the frame from the same clock as the ring: backswing frames spread over the backswing, **Top** on the top tone, downswing frames over the downswing, **Impact** on the impact tone, the follow-through over `throughSeconds`, the finish held until `resetAt`, then Setup for the remainder of the rest
      - The ball is part of the video: it sits at the club at setup and leaves after impact, out of the picture at the frame's edge
      - Every shot type uses one shared size and the same foot position, so the golfer never changes size or jumps when switching shot type. The size is the largest at which every frame of every shot stays inside the ring; the full swing's driver (straight up overhead, wrapped high at the finish) sets it, at 79% of the earlier traced-golfer size
      - **How the frames are made** (sources in `scripts/assets/<shot>/`, built by `scripts/build-swing-sprite.py` into `public/tempo/<shot>.webp` + `components/tempo/swing-sprites.ts`)
        - Four key stills per shot (`key-*.png`): Setup, Top, Impact, Finish, reviewed by Reid before any video is generated
        - Between consecutive stills, 4 s clips generated by Gemini Omni Flash as first-and-last-frame transitions (`clip-*.mp4`)
        - The build script cuts each clip to its one steady stretch, because the clips contain waggles, rehearsal swings and overshoots (the ranges and reasons are commented in `SHOTS`). It samples frames at equal steps of measured motion (optical flow) so the club moves at a steady speed, motion-interpolates slow moves for extra frames, and turns the frames white-on-transparent
        - Interpolation is skipped for fast moves and for the putter, where it draws a ghost second club or makes the putter jump
      - **Full swing** — stills from `full/sheet.png` (poses 1, 5, 8, 10), scaled down in the video frame so the club never leaves it. Backswing from `clip-back.mp4`. Downswing and follow-through are one continuous stretch of `clip-down.mp4`, which carries on past impact into the finish (one frame just after impact, where the club vanishes, is skipped). The club is motion-blurred grey through the follow-through, so grey is drawn solid and the crop keeps the club head where it separates from the blurred shaft. Follow-through 0.5 s, finish held to 1.1 s
      - **Chipping** — stills from `chipping/sheet.png`, the chipping photo sheet converted to silhouettes (Nano Banana; poses 1, 4, 6, 8). In the Setup still the club is rotated about the hands so the head rests against the ball. Three clips; follow-through 0.5 s, finish held to 1.1 s
      - **Putting** — stills from `putting/sheet.png` (poses 1, 7, 10) plus a generated top-of-backstroke still (`key-top.png`; the sheet has none). A putt is a pendulum, so the forward stroke is the backstroke played backwards: the generated forward-stroke clips overshot and drew the putter fading out near impact. The backstroke stops where the putter is furthest back; the clip then drifts forward to the Top still, which looked like a bounce, so that part is cut. Pale putter shafts are kept solid. Follow-through 0.35 s, back at Setup by 0.6 s, inside the fastest metronome beat. Works the same in Metronome mode
      - With Repeat off the rep ends ~0.25 s after impact, cutting the follow-through short
  - Start / Stop button
- **Tempo card**
  - 3-tone: preset chips in a 3-column grid (frame label only) — 5 for full swing, 6 for putting/chipping and per-mode help text
  - Metronome: − / slider / + BPM control
- **Settings card**
  - Repeat toggle (3-tone only) — off plays a single rep then stops
  - Rest between reps: 2 / 3 / 5 s (3-tone only) — also the lead-in before the first rep
  - Volume slider

## Behaviour

- **Tones**: three sine beeps — start 660 Hz, top 880 Hz, impact 1320 Hz. Metronome plays start and impact only.
- **Timing**: beeps are scheduled on the Web Audio clock with a short lookahead (`lib/tempo-audio.ts → TempoEngine`), so spacing does not drift with JS timers. The swing dial reads the same clock.
- **Start** must come from a tap — the `AudioContext` is created/resumed inside the handler (iOS requirement). `navigator.audioSession.type = 'playback'` is set when available so the iOS silent switch does not mute tones.
- **Changing tempo while playing** restarts with the new timing. Volume changes apply live.
- **Stops automatically** when the app is backgrounded, on leaving the page, and after a single rep when Repeat is off.
- **Screen Wake Lock** is held while playing (where supported).
- **Settings persist** in `localStorage` key `tempo-settings`: mode, preset per mode, putting cue, BPM, rest gap, repeat, volume. Invalid stored values fall back to defaults.
